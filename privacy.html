Privacy Policy

ChapterRoll is an attendance utility that stores roster and attendance information locally on the user’s device.

Information handled by the app

ChapterRoll stores the names and badge or roll numbers that a user imports or enters, along with attendance statuses and chapter dates. This information is needed to provide the app’s attendance features.

Data collection and transmission

The current version of ChapterRoll does not transmit roster or attendance information to the developer or any third party. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored locally on the user’s device using Apple-provided storage. Users can delete individual roster entries and attendance sessions or use Delete All Local Data in Settings. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to Apple device backup behavior.

Imported files

ChapterRoll accesses only roster files that the user explicitly selects through Apple’s system document picker. Unused columns such as phone number, email, mailing address, and membership status are not retained by the app.

Children’s privacy

ChapterRoll is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before any version introduces cloud synchronization, accounts, analytics, or other data transmission.
